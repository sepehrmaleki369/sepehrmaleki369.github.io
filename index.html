    Supervisor: Prof. Selim Aksoy

    • Migrated grading from Azure OpenAI to a locally hosted open-weight VLM (Gemma 4 26B MoE, 8-bit, via Ollama on a single RTX 5090) on ~28,000 handwritten answers from ~2,560 students (grades 5–10, five subjects); it matched or outperformed Azure in all 10 grade groups, significantly in 8 (ICC up to 0.95 vs. 0.91).

  2. Faithful Reading of Handwritten Mathematics by Vision-Language Models: Uncertainty and Silent Over-CorrectionJul 2026 – Present
    Supervisor: Prof. Selim Aksoy
    • Developed a training-free, sampling-based uncertainty framework for VLMs reading handwritten math (FERMAT): perception entropy predicts transcription errors (AUROC 0.835 on Qwen2.5-VL-3B, 0.828 on Pixtral-12B), beating token-probability and self-stated confidence (PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, Qwen-VL, Pixtral, InternVL).
    • Identified an evaluation trap in stratified uncertainty evaluation: an apparently strong result (AUROC 0.78–0.85 across three model families) was an artifact, which I retracted and turned into a methodological contribution.
    • Characterized silent over-correction, where VLMs transcribe the correct value instead of the student's error (a 14B grader then misses errors 85% vs. 11% of the time); proposed a training-free detector (real vs. blank-page token probabilities), built a planted-error benchmark from real handwriting, and validated it under pre-registration (AUROC up to 0.77), beating seven recent baselines; paper in preparation (ACL).

Independent Research — Self-directed

  1. Training-Free Epistemic Uncertainty for Medical Image SegmentationJan 2026 – Present
    • Developing a post-hoc method that estimates the epistemic uncertainty of already-trained segmentation networks via data-aware, SVD-based weight perturbation, with no retraining (PyTorch).
    • Benchmarked against 10 methods on 23 shift scenarios across 7 datasets: mean failure-detection AUROC 0.866, matching a 5-network deep ensemble (0.866) and test-time augmentation (0.865), and the most consistent of all 11 methods (worst-case gap 0.035).
    • Ran pre-registered confirmations on untouched data (Prostate158, M&Ms cardiac), which showed no significant advantage over the strongest baselines; found that predicted object size alone explains most methods' signal (AUROC 0.848).
